# GDN chunk kernel provenance

The seven optional SM120 GDN chunk cubins under `bridge/gdn-sm120-matched/`
were generated by the installed SGLang FLA attention implementation.
`bridge/gdn-sm120-matched/manifest.json` records their exact cache digests,
symbols, source roots and hashes. The wrapper source is in `source/gdn2045/`.

SGLang is licensed under Apache License 2.0. Its FLA kernels are adapted from
Flash Linear Attention, licensed under MIT and credited to Songlin Yang and
Yu Zhang in the source files. Copies of the upstream license texts are in this
directory. Upstream projects:

- https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang
- https://github.com/fla-org/flash-linear-attention

The cubins are used only by the opt-in `fast-8k-mtp-gdn` profile.
